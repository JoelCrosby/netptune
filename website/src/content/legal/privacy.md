This policy explains what personal data Netptune collects, why, who it's shared with and what your rights are. It covers the app at app.netptune.co.uk and this website.

## Who we are

Netptune is run by Joel Crosby, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. For your account data and this website, we're the data controller under UK data protection law. For the content a team puts into a workspace, we process it on behalf of that workspace.

Questions and requests go to [support@netptune.co.uk](mailto:support@netptune.co.uk).

## What we collect

- **Account details.** Your name, email address, password (stored only as a hash), profile picture, and your account ID if you sign in with GitHub, Google or Microsoft.
- **Workspace content.** Tasks, pages, comments, reactions, files, automations, saved queries and anything else you or your team add.
- **Assistant conversations.** Your messages, the assistant's replies, and a record of the changes it proposed and you applied. API keys you add are stored encrypted.
- **Activity and security records.** Who changed what and when, including the IP address the change came from, your last sign-in time, and server logs that help us find errors and abuse.
- **Source control data.** If your workspace connects GitHub, we store details of linked repositories, branches, commits, pull requests and check results, and the GitHub usernames on them.
- **Website visits.** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views on this website. It doesn't use cookies or track you across sites.

## How we use it

Each use rests on a lawful basis under UK GDPR:

- **Contract.** Running your account and workspaces, including search, notifications and the emails you've asked for, and the assistant, integrations and imports you switch on.
- **Legitimate interests.** Keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, fixing problems, and telling you about important changes to the service or these policies.
- **Legal obligation.** Meeting our legal and tax obligations.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train AI models.

## Who we share it with

We use these providers to run Netptune. They only process data on our instructions.

| Provider                  | What for                                                             |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Vultr                     | Servers and databases, in Manchester, UK                             |
| Amazon Web Services       | File storage and database backups                                    |
| Cloudflare                | Network delivery, security, bot checks at sign-in, website analytics |
| Twilio SendGrid           | Sending email                                                        |
| GitHub, Google, Microsoft | Signing you in, if you choose that option                            |
| GitHub                    | Linking code to tasks, if your workspace connects it                 |

Some services only receive data when you or your workspace switch them on:

- **Anthropic or OpenAI**, when you use the assistant. What the assistant needs to answer is sent under the API key you or your workspace provided, and that provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.
- **Brave, Google or a SearXNG instance your workspace chooses**, if your workspace configures web search for the assistant. Only the search query is sent.

During an [import](/docs/import-export), the assistant can be sent a few sample cell values to help match up columns. Administrators can turn that off so only column names are sent.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

## International transfers

Our servers and databases are in the UK. Some providers process data outside the UK, mainly in the United States. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised by UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

## Cookies and local storage

The app uses two essential cookies to keep you signed in: `access_token` and `refresh_token`. It also stores your display settings and sign-in state in your browser's local storage, which is cleared when you sign out. Cloudflare's bot check on the sign-in and registration forms may set a cookie it needs to work.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't ask for cookie consent.

## How long we keep it

- Account details and workspace content are kept while your account or workspace exists.
- When you delete an assistant conversation, it disappears for you straight away but stays in our database marked as deleted. Ask us if you want it removed for good.
- Activity records are kept for as long as the workspace exists, because they're its history.
- Database backups roll over and are kept for a limited time, so deleted data can remain in them until they expire.

When you ask us to close your account, we delete your account details and remove you from your workspaces. Workspaces that other people still use are kept, along with what you contributed to them, unless their owner deletes them.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your personal data
- correct it
- delete it
- restrict or object to how we use it
- send it to you in a portable format

Email [support@netptune.co.uk](mailto:support@netptune.co.uk) and we'll reply within a month. You can also export your workspace yourself at any time.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk). We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

## Children

Netptune isn't meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
